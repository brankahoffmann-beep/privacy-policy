Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Android application Aha! Habits, published by Hoffmann Software ("we", "our", or "us"). Aha! Habits is a task and habit management app that stores your data locally on your device and is designed with privacy in mind.

Data Collection

This app does not collect, store, or share personal data.

Local Data Storage

All tasks, tags and other app data is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to any servers.

Location & Weather

If you choose to use weather-based features and grant location permission, the app may temporarily access your device's location to retrieve local weather information. Your location is used only for this purpose and is not stored by Hoffmann Software.

Third-Party Services

Weather data is provided by a third-party weather service. To retrieve weather data, the app sends only the information required to process the request. Hoffmann Software does not collect or store this information.
